About me

I am currently completing my Bachelor's degree in Data Science at FHNW. My interests include machine learning, medical image processing, computational morphology, and applied algorithm design.

Bachelor Thesis

My bachelor thesis focuses on the reconstruction of fractured wrist joint surfaces, specifically the distal radius head. The goal of the project is to algorithmically determine how fragmented bone pieces can be repositioned into their anatomically correct locations. This approach supports orthopedic surgeons during fracture treatment by helping them identify where each fragment should be aligned before fixation. The code however, is not publicly available due to privacy regulations concerning medical data.

This work includes:

  • automatic landmark detection
  • reconstruction of target morphology
  • fragment matching and geometric alignment
  • evaluation of reconstructed bone surfaces
